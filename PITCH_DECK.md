---

## Slide 2: The Problem
### **The Web2 Scheduling Tax**
* **Monthly Subscriptions:** Traditional platforms charge $15–$50/month just to share a booking link.
* **Privacy Intrusions (PII Extraction):** Clients are forced to surrender personal data (emails, phone numbers, trackers).
* **Double Bookings & Sync Lags:** Centralized databases suffer from delayed syncs, leading to double-booked slots.
* **Deplatforming & Lock-In:** Providers don't own their booking history or client relationships.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
### **Booklio: Trustless, Decentralized Scheduling**
* **On-Chain Immutability:** Services, available slots, and bookings live transparently on the Botchain EVM.
* **Smart Double-Booking Lock:** Contract logic prevents double bookings at the protocol level.
* **Zero PII Required:** Connect via MetaMask—no email, phone number, or invasive tracking needed.
* **Zero Middlemen:** Direct provider-to-client interaction with zero platform subscription fees.

---

## Slide 5: Product Features & Architecture
### **Designed for Simplicity & Speed**
* **Dashboard Overview:** Real-time statistics showing active services, open slots, and confirmed appointments.
* **Service Directory:** Browse and filter services published directly by verified providers.
* **Interactive Slot Browser:** View slots converted to the client's local timezone with one-click booking.
* **Lifecycle Management:** Dedicated portals for confirming pending bookings or cancelling with auto-reopened slots.
* **Tech Stack:** Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Solidity ^0.8.24, Hardhat, wagmi v2, viem.
